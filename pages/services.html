Certified Protection

Warranty & Product Protection

Our warranty coverage depends on the specific product line and its stated warranty terms. We ensure transparent support for genuine manufacturing defects throughout the specified period.

Warranty Overview

Every authentic AeroPack item includes product-specific coverage against material failures and manufacturing craftsmanship anomalies under normal travel usage.

Manufacturing Defect Support

Coverage specifically addresses seam separation, shell cracking under normal usage, zipper slider malfunctions, and factory lock mechanism failures.

Warranty Claim Enquiry Process

Submit an enquiry via our Contact page with your model name, high-resolution photographs of the affected component, and description of the issue.

Proof-of-Purchase Guidance

Keep your original digital invoice, store receipt, or online order confirmation email as proof of acquisition to expedite warranty validation.

Product Care Recommendations

Clean polycarbonate shells using mild soapy lukewarm water and a microfiber cloth. Avoid solvent-based abrasive chemicals that could degrade the UV outer coating.

Coverage Disclaimers

Warranty terms do not cover normal aesthetic scuffs, third-party airline baggage handler maltreatment, transport accidents, or unauthorized modifications.

Restoration & Maintenance

Luggage Repair & After-Sales Services

Extend the lifespan of your luggage. All repair services are subject to physical component assessment and actual service parts availability.

Wheel Replacement Enquiries

Inquire about replacement Hinomoto dual-bearing spinner wheel modules and caster assemblies compatible with your specific generation.

Handle Repair Enquiries

Telescopic pull-handle tube realignments, button release lock servicing, and top/side cushioned carry handle replacements.

Zipper Repair Enquiries

Assessment of damaged reverse-coil zipper teeth, slider replacements, and puller cord re-attachments for weather-sealed models.

Lock-Related Support

Guidance for resetting TSA 3-digit combination locks, key cylinder evaluations, and replacement recessed lock housings.

Shell & Fabric Assessment

Technical evaluation of exterior polycarbonate impact depressions or ballistic weave fabric tears to determine structural repairability.

Spare Parts & Self-Repair Kits

Request authentic screws, interior compression straps, and modular caster parts for straightforward home installation.
