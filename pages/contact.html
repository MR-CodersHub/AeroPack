Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding sizing, warranties, repair processes, and international shipping.

Most international airlines adhering to IATA recommendations allow carry-on baggage dimensions up to 55 x 35 x 23 cm (approx. 21.5 x 14 x 9 inches), including wheels and handles. Our Stratus Luxe Cabin Suitcase is designed strictly within these measurements to ensure compliant fit in overhead lockers across North America, Europe, and Asia.
Our warranty specifically covers functional manufacturing defects in the polycarbonate shell, aluminum telescopic pull handles, wheel casters, and zippers under standard travel usage. It does not cover cosmetic scuffs, airline mishandling (which must be filed with the carrier at the airport), or unauthorized modifications.
Yes! Every AeroPack suitcase is engineered with modular hardware. Our Hinomoto silent wheel casters and telescopic handle tubes can be easily detached and replaced using a standard screwdriver. Replacement kits with step-by-step video instructions can be requested through our repair enquiry service.
All AeroPack locks arrive preset to factory default 0-0-0. To set your personal code: push the small reset button with a ballpoint pen until it clicks, roll the dials to your preferred 3 digits, then slide the opening latch. The reset button will click back into place, securing your new combination.
Yes, AeroPack ships to over 85 countries worldwide. Full 2-piece and 3-piece luggage sets qualify for complimentary express freight. Estimated transit times range from 2 to 4 business days for North America and Western Europe, and 4 to 7 business days for other global territories.
