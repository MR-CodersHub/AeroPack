Design Narrative

Product Description & Intended Use

Engineered for Seamless Transit

The AeroVoyager Pro 3-Piece Luggage Set was conceived for travelers who refuse to compromise between structural resilience, packing capacity, and airport aesthetic poise. Whether boarding a 45-minute regional hopper or packing for a two-month transatlantic sabbatical, this tri-part suite equips you for every itinerary conceivable.

Each trunk is pressed from 100% Bayer Makrolon® polycarbonate, producing a featherweight shell that actively absorbs high impacts in baggage sorting facilities and springs back into shape without denting or cracking.

When not traveling, all three pieces nest seamlessly inside the 28" trunk like Russian nesting dolls, taking up minimal closet space at home.

Travel Suitability Overview

  • 20" Cabin Spinner: 1-4 day business trips & weekend getaways. Complies with IATA major airline overhead racks.
  • 24" Medium Trunk: 5-9 day leisure journeys, road trips, and international connections requiring checked baggage.
  • 28" Extended Trunk: 10+ day international voyages, family holidays, winter expeditions, or study abroad semesters.
  • Nesting Footprint: Store all 3 pieces within a single 52 x 32 cm footprint when parked at home.

Engineered Innovations

Key Features & Design Highlights

Explore the specialized internal and external components that distinguish AeroPack from standard retail luggage.

360° Whisper-Glide Movement

Hinomoto dual-wheel casters rotate silently in all directions, dampening acoustic vibrations by 75% on terminal stone tiles.

3-Stage Telescopic Handle

Precision aluminum tube locks securely at 95 cm, 100 cm, and 105 cm heights to accommodate travelers of any stature.

Dual Clamshell Organization

One side features a zippered mesh divider with waterproof toiletry pocket; the opposite side employs a buckled compression pad.

Reinforced Corner Caps

Riveted aluminum alloy corner shields protect vulnerable edges against violent baggage conveyor drops.

TSA Recessed Security Lock

Flush-mounted keyless 3-digit combination lock allows airport security agents to inspect without breaking your zipper pullers.

Self-Retracting Carry Handles

Damped silicone-cushioned grab handles on both top and side automatically fold flat against the shell when released.

Official Maintenance Checklist

  • Wheel Servicing: Periodically check wheel axels and remove accumulated lint or dust with a dry brush.
  • Zipper Lubrication: Use a dry paraffin wax pencil along the zipper coils once every six months.
  • Storage: Store the suitcases nested in a dry, ventilated closet away from direct UV sunlight.
  • Spare Parts: Hinomoto replacement wheels and handle modules are stocked and available through our repair service.
