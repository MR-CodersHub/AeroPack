The Inception

Born from Broken Wheels & Frustrated Layovers

In 2018, after enduring cracked suitcase shells on an icy Munich tarmac and a shattered wheel assembly in Tokyo Station, our industrial design team founded AeroPack with a single obsession: build travel luggage that never fails when you are thousands of miles from home.

We eliminated decorative clutter and focused purely on mechanical integrity—pairing virgin German polycarbonate with silent dual-bearing casters and zero-rattle aircraft aluminum.

Today, AeroPack accompanies business executives, diplomats, digital nomads, and families across more than 85 nations worldwide.

Rigorous Testing

Product Design & Quality Standards

Before any AeroPack suitcase or travel bag enters your hands, prototype units must conquer destructive multi-stage laboratory simulations.

LAB-01 100% Virgin Shell

Aerospace Polycarbonate Shells

Engineered from 100% Bayer Makrolon® polycarbonate that yields under violent cargo compression and instantly rebounds to its pristine contour.

LAB-02 -15°C / 25kg Load

Tumble & Free-Fall Drop Trials

Full 25-kilogram loaded suitcases undergo 50 free-fall drops onto steel spikes at -15°C to certify corner integrity and latch endurance.

LAB-03 50km Endurance

50km High-Speed Treadmill Test

Wheel casters are pushed over stepped steel obstacle belts for 50 uninterrupted kilometers to verify silent, friction-free rotational glide.

LAB-04 10,000 Cycles

10,000-Cycle Handle Jerk Test

Telescopic aluminum handles are mechanically extended and repeatedly yanked with full loads to guarantee latch pins never jam.

LAB-05 Hydro-Shield

Monsoon Water Ingress Chamber

Water-repellent ballistic shells and reverse-coil coated zipper tracks undergo high-pressure water spray tests to keep garments dry.

LAB-06 32-Point Audit

Micro-Inspection Quality Control

Every unit receives manual 32-point inspection covering zipper puller alignment, TSA lock resets, and internal lining stitching tension.
