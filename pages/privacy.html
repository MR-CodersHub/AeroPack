Data Protection • Privacy Standards

Global Privacy Policy & Confidentiality

At AeroPack, we protect your personal travel data with the same uncompromising rigor and precision we engineer into our aerospace-grade suitcases.

01. Information Taxonomy

Data We Collect & Process

Information You Provide

When submitting warranty repair forms, booking concierge inquiries, or joining our travel journal newsletter, we collect your name, email, phone number, shipping address, and proof of purchase documents.

Client-Side Preferences

We store your shopping cart contents, saved wishlist luggage items, dark/light theme choice, and RTL layout selection strictly in your browser's LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device unprompted.

02. Purposeful Processing

How Your Data Powers Seamless Travel

  • Fulfillment & Dispatch: Coordinating with certified couriers to deliver carry-on suitcases and replacement Hinomoto wheel sets to your exact home or hotel address.
  • Warranty Registration & Traceability: Maintaining serial number records for our limited lifetime warranties to ensure rapid replacement during verified defect assessments.
  • Concierge Communication: Responding directly to your sizing, airline cabin compliance, or international freight queries.
  • Zero Data Monetization: We never sell, lease, or broker your personal information to third-party ad brokers or data aggregators.
03. Local Technologies

Cookies, LocalStorage & Analytics

Essential Storage

Persists cart items across page navigations without requiring account logins.

UI State Memory

Remembers your dark mode preference and RTL/LTR reading configuration.

Zero Third-Party Trackers

No intrusive advertising pixels or behavioral profiling beacons.

04. Sovereign Control

Your Rights Under GDPR, CCPA & Global Standards

Regardless of your citizenship or destination airport, AeroPack affords all global voyagers universal privacy protections:

Right to Access & Portability

You may request an export of all warranty and order records associated with your email address.

Right to Erasure ("To Be Forgotten")

You can request full deletion of inquiry communications and marketing newsletter subscriptions.

Right to Rectification

Update your registered delivery destination or contact details at any point prior to carrier dispatch.

Right to Non-Discrimination

Exercising your privacy rights will never alter product pricing, warranty support, or concierge priority.

Data Protection Officer • AeroPack Global

Exercise Your Privacy Rights

To request data deletion, verify stored warranty records, or consult our Data Protection Officer regarding cross-border compliance, please contact our privacy desk.
