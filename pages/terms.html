04. Protection Standard

Warranty Scope & Repair Conditions

Every AeroPack suitcase is engineered to rigorous standards. As outlined on our Services & Warranty page, our limited warranty protects against manufacturing defects in polycarbonate shells, telescopic handles, and wheel assemblies.

  • Covered: Cracks in the luggage shell rendering it unusable, failed stitching on load points, seized Hinomoto casters, and broken TSA lock mechanisms.
  • Excluded: Surface scratches, cosmetic scuffs, chemical stains, third-party airline mishandling (which must be filed directly with your airline baggage counter via Property Irregularity Report), or unauthorized modifications.
  • Repair Protocol: Approved warranty claims receive OEM spare parts or factory refurbishing at our authorized technical centers.
